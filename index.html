2. Scoring methodology

Overall scoreKuWork user intent score × 60% + AI intent score × 40%, used to prioritize the 17 prompts.
User intent scoreBased on collected KuWork user statements and selection signals, normalized to 0–1 to reflect demand strength.
AI intent scoreBased on intent recognition for KuWork prompts in AI conversations across four platforms, normalized to 0–1 to reflect intent alignment.
Overall scoreKuailu Office user intent score × 60% + AI intent score × 40%, used to prioritize the 20 prompts.
User intent scoreBased on user statements and selection signals collected in both Kuailu Office batches, normalized to 0–1 to reflect demand strength.
AI intent scoreBased on intent recognition for Kuailu Office prompts in AI conversations across four platforms, normalized to 0–1 to reflect intent alignment.

3. Field definitions

Core scenarioA major office decision topic within the KuWork product line, such as agent building, deployment and integration, or data security.
Core intentThe main decision a KuWork user makes within a core scenario; all intents in this map are classified as brand selection.
Specific concernsA more detailed breakdown of a KuWork core intent, describing specific user concerns, goals, or usage requirements.
Core scenarioA major office decision topic within the Kuailu Office product line, such as data security, system integration, or automation.

Specific concernsA more detailed breakdown of a Kuailu Office core intent, describing specific user concerns, goals, or usage requirements.
